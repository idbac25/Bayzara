## 1. My role (the user)

I am acting as an **independent economic evaluator**. I am **not** an advisor to either party.
My loyalty is to **method and standards**, not to any party's outcome. All analysis must be
**neutral and applied symmetrically to both sides**.

My mandate has **two phases, in strict order**:

1. **Phase 1 — Technical / methodology evaluation:** evaluate the *evaluation methods* against
   world (international) standards. You do not validate a number produced by a method you have
   not first validated.
2. **Phase 2 — Financial evaluation:** only after the method passes Phase 1, derive and test the
   actual financial values, ranges, and sensitivities, and report an independent range.

**Principle: methods first, numbers second.**

## 2. The transaction being evaluated

A proposed **merger / joint venture** between:

- **MWSC — Mogadishu (a.k.a. "Moktoshu") Water Supply Company** — the target/operator.
- **BECO (the user sometimes writes "BECCO" / "Beko")** — the acquirer.

### BECO — who they are (verified via web search)
- **BECO = Banadir (Banaadir) Electric Company**, Somalia's **largest electricity utility**.
- Founded **5 May 2014** by **merging the major electricity companies of the Banadir region** —
  i.e. BECO is a **serial consolidator** with a repeatable absorption playbook.
- Covers ~**80% of Mogadishu**, the airport/Halane, plus Jubaland, Southwest, Hirshabelle;
  distributes power to **Kismayo, Barawe, Marka, Balad, Jowhar, Afgooye, Elasha, Mogadishu**.
- Well-capitalized: **$220M green-energy program**, ~30% renewables; solar cut tariff from
  **$0.49 → $0.36/kWh**.
- **It is a power company expanding sideways into water** (a new vertical), NOT a water company.
- Sources: beco.so; Banaadir Electricity Company–BECO (LinkedIn); The Star (Mar 2026).

### Deal structure facts (as told by the user — confirm/keep neutral)
- **Equity split is a FIXED control rule:** BECO always takes **51%**, MWSC keeps **49%**.
  The split is **predetermined — NOT derived from the asset valuation.**
  (→ An earlier "30% haircut is a control lever / you'd otherwise own 58%" line of reasoning was
  **withdrawn** once this was clarified. The split does not follow the valuation.)
- The **49%** sits in a **ring-fenced MWSC subsidiary only** — MWSC shareholders do **not**
  share in BECO's wider group upside.
- BECO applies a **30% downward adjustment ("haircut") to MWSC's assets only** (asymmetric /
  one-sided) — basis unknown.
- BECO offers a discretionary **~$3M "goodwill" sweetener**.
- **BECO's own contribution for its 51% is still UNKNOWN** (cash? folding in assets? capital +
  management?) — open question to confirm.

### Key strategic/contextual reads (kept neutral for an evaluator)
- Because the split is fixed, the question becomes: **what does the 30% haircut actually adjust?**
  Candidates: (a) recorded capital accounts of the subsidiary, (b) the fairness basis for the
  $3M, or (c) a future buyout/exit formula for the 49%. **Ask: "Since the split is fixed, what
  does the 30% adjust?"** If "nothing, just bookkeeping," it has no analytical basis.
- **Related-party risk to note (neutral disclosure):** BECO would be both the **controlling
  parent AND the electricity supplier** to the water subsidiary → transfer-pricing / management-
  fee channels could move profit from the 49%-shared sub to the 100%-owned parent. Methodology
  must disclose/handle this.
- **Synergy of record:** BECO already powers **Afgooye/Elasha (where MWSC's wellfield sits)**.
  Pumping energy (diesel) is a water utility's biggest OPEX; BECO's (solar) grid could slash it.
- BECO's **template was built for absorbing small diesel electricity gensets** (commodity,
  replaceable) — MWSC is a strategic, hard-to-replicate aquifer-based system; a neutral
  methodology should not assume the template fits.

## 3. Ownership / legal situation (critical scoping fact)

- After the **1991 collapse of the central government**, MWSC has for **~35 years** managed,
  maintained, protected, and **added substantial value** to a formerly **public** water system.
- **There is NO formal contract/concession.** The government has only **tacit approval** for
  MWSC to operate.
- Therefore **legal title is unresolved** — the evaluator must NOT assume either extreme
  ("all public" vs "all MWSC's"). (This partly walked back an earlier "it's all public / IPSAS
  governs the lot" framing.)

### Neutral valuation treatment agreed
1. **Separate value from ownership.** Value the asset base by DRC / income approach regardless of
   owner; attribute ownership per a **stated special assumption (IVS 101/102)** taken from a
   **qualified Somali-law title opinion (IVS 104 reliance on a legal specialist)** — the
   evaluator does not adjudicate title.
2. **Asset vintage split:** after 35 years, most pre-1991 physical assets are depreciated or
   physically replaced; **most in-service assets were likely MWSC-funded.** Date each asset by
   class and let depreciation reveal the split (evidence-based, neutral).
3. **Quantify "value added" in three buckets:** (a) MWSC self-funded capex/betterment (cost
   approach), (b) going concern & intangibles — customer base, billing/collections, operating
   capability, brand (**IVS 210** / income approach), (c) potential **improvement/unjust-
   enrichment compensation claim** if title reverts (subject to legal opinion).
4. **Title-risk discount:** a purely tacit, revocable operating right is fragile → reflected
   **symmetrically** as an income-approach risk input (horizon/discount rate), applied to the
   whole enterprise regardless of which party holds it.
5. **Present value under multiple title scenarios** so the conclusion is transparent, not hostage
   to an unresolved legal question.

**Open fork:** Does a written Somali-law title/legal opinion exist or can one be commissioned?
This determines whether the methodology presents a single title basis or a multi-scenario matrix.

## 4. Applicable world standards (the benchmark set)

### Valuation
- **IVS 2025** (International Valuation Standards, effective 31 Jan 2025): IVS 101 Scope of Work,
  IVS 102 Bases of Value, IVS 103 Valuation Approaches, IVS 104 Data & Inputs, IVS 105 Valuation
  Models, IVS 106 Documentation & Reporting; **IVS 200 Businesses & Business Interests**,
  **IVS 210 Intangible Assets**.
  - IVS 102 **Equitable Value** likely more appropriate than Market Value for a transfer between
    two specific identified parties (BECO & MWSC).
  - IVS 200: cost approach is "rarely applicable" to a cash-generating business/concession →
    **income approach (DCF)** normally primary there; **DRC (cost approach)** is for specialized
    physical assets with no active market.
- **IPSAS 45 Property, Plant & Equipment** (effective 1 Jan 2025, replaced IPSAS 17): governs
  **public-sector infrastructure** (explicitly names water supply systems); recognizes
  **Depreciated Replacement Cost (DRC)** / current operational value. **Applies only to whatever
  portion is genuinely public.**
- **RICS Red Book Global** — operationalizes IVS (supporting good practice).

### Borehole / water engineering (Phase 1 technical, for the asset assessment)
- **ISO 14686** — Hydrometric determinations: pumping tests for water wells *(cite edition to be
  verified)*.
- **Kruseman & de Ridder, *Analysis and Evaluation of Pumping Test Data* (ILRI Pub. 47)** —
  global reference for pumping-test analysis.
- **AWWA A100** — Standard for Water Wells (construction/condition) *(cite edition to be
  verified)*.
- **WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality, 4th ed. incl. 1st & 2nd addenda (2022)** +
  **Water Safety Plan** framework — the water-quality authority.
- **US EPA borehole geophysics** methods; **USGS** groundwater sustainability & seawater
  intrusion programs.

## 5. Phase 1 methodology-conformance checklist (apply symmetrically to BOTH parties)

1. **Object of valuation (IVS 101):** carve estate into *original public asset / MWSC-funded
   improvements / intangibles & going concern*.
2. **Basis of value (IVS 102):** declare & justify (Equitable Value likely); state title special
   assumption(s).
3. **Approach selection (IVS 103/105, IVS 200):** is DRC right per asset class? Income approach
   for the revenue-earning concession?
4. **Consistency & symmetry:** are the same methods/assumptions/adjustments applied to both
   sides? (The **30% one-sided haircut** is a consistency/objectivity test, not a grievance.)
5. **Data & inputs (IVS 104):** replacement costs, useful lives, depreciation sourced & reliable;
   reliance on legal title opinion noted.
6. **Documentation (IVS 106):** transparent, reproducible.
7. **Independence/objectivity:** disclose related-party features (BECO controlling + supplying;
   political ties) as risk factors.

**Phase-1 deliverable:** a methodology-conformance scorecard rating the approach against IVS 2025
+ IPSAS 45 (conforms / partial / non-conforming), with the gap and standard reference for each.

## 6. Borehole & Well Evaluation Plan (CURRENT WORKSTREAM)

Context: **coastal, semi-arid aquifer; MWSC wellfield near Afgooye.** Lead value drivers are
**(a) sustainable yield vs over-abstraction** and **(b) saltwater intrusion / salinity trend**.
A structurally perfect borehole is worth little if water turns brackish or the table is falling.

**Per-module template: Authority → Method → Metric → Valuation input.**

- **Module 1 — Construction & Structural Integrity.** *Authority:* AWWA A100, US EPA borehole
  geophysics. *Methods:* CCTV/downhole camera; caliper log; gamma/casing logs; verticality;
  wellhead/sanitary-seal inspection. *Metrics:* casing wall loss/corrosion, screen open-area
  loss, encrustation, collapse zones, seal integrity. *Feeds:* remaining structural life → DRC
  depreciation; rehab-vs-replace cost. (No as-builts here → camera+caliper reconstructs records.)
- **Module 2 — Hydraulic Performance & Yield.** *Authority:* ISO 14686; Kruseman & de Ridder;
  Kenya Water Authority Code of Practice for Test Pumping (regional template). *Methods:*
  step-drawdown (3+ rates ≥60 min) → well efficiency / well vs aquifer loss; constant-rate
  (±5%, 24–72 h) → sustainable yield + aquifer params; recovery test → cross-check T. *Metrics:*
  specific capacity (Q/drawdown), sustainable yield (L/s, m³/day), efficiency %, transmissivity
  (T), storativity (S). *Feeds:* revenue capacity; ageing indicator (specific-capacity decline).
- **Module 3 — Aquifer Sustainability ("will it dry up?").** *Authority:* USGS sustainability
  framework (sustainable yield, not static "safe yield"). *Methods:* long-term water-level
  monitoring (dataloggers, m/yr trend); recharge / water-balance; T & S scaled to wellfield;
  well-interference / combined drawdown. *Metrics:* static-level trend, recharge vs abstraction,
  regional drawdown. *Feeds:* asset life horizon (income approach); over-abstraction risk flag /
  discount-rate driver.
- **Module 4 — Water Quality.** *Authority:* WHO GDWQ 4th ed. + addenda (2022); Water Safety
  Plan. *Methods:* lab panel on representative + seasonal samples. *Core values:* E. coli /
  thermotolerant coliforms = 0/100 mL; arsenic 0.01 mg/L; fluoride 1.5 mg/L; nitrate 50 mg/L
  (as NO₃); **salinity suite: TDS, EC, chloride, sodium, sulfate**. *Feeds:* treatment OPEX;
  salinity trend caps price & life.
- **Module 5 — Saltwater Intrusion (coastal overlay on M3 & M4).** *Authority:* USGS Seawater
  Intrusion; Ghyben–Herzberg basal-lens principle. *Methods:* EC depth profiling; chloride trend
  monitoring; resistivity mapping of fresh/salt interface; watch upward intrusion from
  over-pumping & vertical migration via corroded/abandoned wells (ties to M1). *Metrics:*
  chloride/EC trend (mg/L/yr), depth to interface, advance toward wellfield. *Feeds:* potentially
  the single largest impairment.

**Synthesis → Remaining Useful Life (RUL) & risk grade per borehole:**
- **RUL = minimum of** structural life (M1), aquifer life (M3), quality/salinity life (M4–M5).
- **Capacity = sustainable yield** (M2), de-rated for blending losses.
- **OPEX = pumping energy + treatment** (M4) — note BECO power synergy.
- RUL+capacity+OPEX → income approach; RUL+condition → DRC depreciation.

**Tiered execution (many boreholes):**
1. **Tier 1 — Census (all):** ID, location, depth, construction date, casing/screen spec, pump,
   metered output, static level + EC spot.
2. **Tier 2 — Rapid field screen (all):** wellhead condition, water level, EC/salinity spot,
   specific-capacity spot check.
3. **Tier 3 — Full assessment (representative + critical subset):** CCTV+caliper, full
   constant-rate pumping test, full WHO lab panel, EC depth profile. Extrapolate to population.
