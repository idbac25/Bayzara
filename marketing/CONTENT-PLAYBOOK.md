# Tocktify Content Playbook

Somali-first, English-subtitled. Two TikTok posts per day (1:00pm and 8:30pm
EAT — the second slot catches Somalia prime time AND US-diaspora midday).
Everything cross-posted to YouTube Shorts. One long YouTube upload per week
(documentary part, Fridays 7pm EAT).

---

## 1. Content pillars and weekly mix (14 TikToks/week)

| Pillar | /wk | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Seller stories** | 4 | Ahmed (Banana Bully) & Sahel Drops day-in-the-life, order packing, first-order reactions; later, every new Founding 100 seller's "store goes live" moment |
| **The enemy is the DM** | 3 | Relatable pain content: payment-screenshot chaos, lost orders, "sariirta warbaahinta" selling. Skits work — Somali TikTok loves comedy |
| **Education / tutorials** | 3 | 60-sec how-tos: upload a product, create a tracking link, get paid in 24h; plus "how to get a business license" series (from the onboarding SOP) |
| **Building in public / founder** | 2 | CEO updates: "Week 3: 47 sellers onboarded", behind the scenes of the team, answering hard questions (fees, trust, payouts) |
| **Suuq Live clips** | 2–3 | Best moments from Thursday's live |

Rules that compound:
- **Reply to every comment; turn the best questions into video replies** —
  TikTok's video-reply feature is the cheapest content ideation engine.
- Every video ends with ONE CTA only: link in bio → waitlist. Founding 100
  videos CTA → application.
- Hook in the first 1.5 seconds, in Somali, text on screen.
- Caption format: Somali hook + English line + 3–5 hashtags
  (#somalitiktok #somalibusiness #ganacsi #xamar #soomaaliya + city tags
  #minneapolis #eastleigh when targeting diaspora).

## 2. Ten ready-to-shoot scripts

**S1 — "Screenshot-kii lacagta" (the payment screenshot)** *(Enemy-of-DM, skit)*
Hook: *"Walaal, screenshot-ka lacagta ii soo dir…" — 47 times a day?*
Seller scrolling endless WhatsApp payment screenshots, losing one, customer
angry. Cut: same seller opens Tocktify dashboard — every order, every
payment, automatically. *"Joojiy ganacsiga DM-ka. Tocktify.com — link bio."*

**S2 — Ahmed's 6am cake order** *(Seller story)*
Hook: *Mid ayaa Mareykanka ka soo dalbaday keeg — habeenkii.*
("Someone in America ordered a cake — at night.") Ahmed wakes to an order
notification placed from Minneapolis, bakes it, delivers to the buyer's
family in Mogadishu, films the family receiving it. CTA: *"Qof aad jeceshahay
keeg ugu dir — tocktify.com."* — This is the diaspora-wedge video; boost
hard in Minneapolis/London/Toronto.

**S3 — "24 saac kadib lacagtaada"** *(Education)*
Hook: *Goorma ayaad lacagtaada heshaa? 24 saac. Mar walba.*
Screen-record the payout flow: sale happens → countdown → EVC Plus message
arrives on the seller's phone. Show the actual SMS. Trust content — in this
market, *seeing the payout SMS* is worth 100 testimonials.

**S4 — Founding 100 announcement** *(Founder, boost this)*
Hook: *100 qof oo kaliya. Waxaan kuu samaynaynaa wax aysan Shopify abid kuu
samayn karin.* ("Only 100 people. We'll do for you what Shopify never
could.") CEO to camera: we get you the license, the Hormuud merchant
account, the store, the payouts — done with you, founding price locked.
CTA: apply, link in bio.

**S5 — "Maxaad iibin lahayd?" giveaway** *(UGC engine)*
Hook: *Sanad bilaash ah oo Tocktify — hal su'aal.* ("A free year of
Tocktify — one question.") Stitch/duet this video telling us what you'd
sell online; best answer wins a free year (list value $300, cash cost ~$0).
Pin it; boost it; it generates an audience-map of what people want to sell.

**S6 — License myth-buster** *(Education series, 3 parts)*
Hook: *"Ruqsad ganacsi ma leedahay?" — Ha ka baqin.* ("'Do you have a
business license?' — Don't fear it.") Employee C walks through exactly what
a license costs, how long it takes, what Tocktify handles for you. The
licensing fear is the silent objection — name it on camera.

**S7 — Sahel Drops restock rush** *(Seller story)*
Time-lapse of Sahel packing grocery orders with order-count overlay,
*"Maanta: 23 dalab"* style. No revenue numbers (per their preference) —
order counts and busy hands tell the story.

**S8 — "Dukaan 5 daqiiqo gudahood" (a store in 5 minutes)** *(Education / demo-store launch)*
Screen recording, phone-only: sign up → name your store → upload one
product → share link to WhatsApp status. Ends: *"Bilaash ku tijaabi —
demo store. Lacag-qaadista markaad diyaar tahay."* (Try free in demo mode;
activate payments when ready.)

**S9 — Diaspora call-out** *(Diaspora, English-led)*
Hook: *"Your mom in Xamar runs a business from her phone. She just can't
take payments online. Until now."* English with Somali subs, boost only in
diaspora cities. CTA: *"Tag her. Or set up her store yourself —
tocktify.com."* Tag-a-relative CTAs have built-in virality.

**S10 — Weekly scoreboard** *(Building in public)*
Every Monday: CEO, 30 seconds, whiteboard: *"Toddobaadkan: X seller cusub,
Y dalab, Z magaalo."* (This week: X new sellers, Y orders, Z cities.)
Running public momentum makes followers feel like investors.

## 3. YouTube plan

- **Fridays 7pm EAT:** documentary parts 2, 3, 4 on consecutive Fridays
  (Somali + English versions like part 1). Boost each $20 to East Africa +
  diaspora geos. After part 4, the Friday slot becomes "Founding 100
  stories" — 5–8 min mini-docs of new sellers (the documentary format you
  already proved, industrialized).
- **Shorts daily** (repurposed TikToks — re-export without TikTok watermark).
- **Tutorials playlist** ("Tocktify Academy"): the S6/S8-style videos in
  full length; this becomes your onboarding manual, cutting Employee C's
  support load.
- Subscriber boosts: fine to continue at trickle spend, but optimize for
  East Africa + diaspora; non-Somali East African subs are future-market
  seeding (Kenya next), not waste — just don't pay much for them yet.

## 4. Suuq Live (every Thursday 8:30pm EAT)

Rundown: 5 min welcome + week's numbers → 10 min live demo / new feature →
20 min guest seller interview → 20 min open Q&A from comments → 5 min CTA
(waitlist / Founding 100). Clip 3–5 shorts next morning. Guests weeks 1–2:
Ahmed, Sahel; week 3+: newest Founding 100 sellers.

## 5. Boost playbook (TikTok)

1. Post organic. Wait 24–48h.
2. Boost only the top ⅓ by completion rate + comments — $1–2/day × 6–7 days.
3. Always-boost list regardless of organic performance: S2 (diaspora wedge),
   S4 (Founding 100), S5 (giveaway), each documentary trailer.
4. Two audience buckets per boosted video where possible: Somalia-reachable
   + one diaspora city. Compare cost-per-waitlist-signup, not views.
5. Log every boost in a sheet: video, spend, views, profile visits, link
   clicks, signups. Monday review; kill >$0.50/signup, scale <$0.20.

## 6. Zero-cost distribution (do not skip)

- **WhatsApp statuses** — every team member, Ahmed, Sahel post store links
  and clips to status; in Somalia WhatsApp status is a top-3 channel.
- **Facebook groups** — Somali buy/sell groups (Mogadishu, Hargeisa,
  diaspora city groups) are huge; share seller stories (not ads) + the
  free guide. 3 posts/week, value-first.
- **Somali business TikTokers/YouTubers** — offer free year + revenue
  share via referral link instead of cash. 5 outreaches/week. Micro
  (5–50K followers) converts better than celebrities and costs nothing
  up front.
- **Duet/stitch** trending Somali small-business content daily — free
  distribution on other people's reach.
