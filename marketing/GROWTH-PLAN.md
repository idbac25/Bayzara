# Tocktify Growth Plan — June 11 → August 1, 2026

**Owner:** CEO + 3-person team
**Cash budget:** $500 (boosts + incentives allowed)
**Channels:** TikTok (@tocktify.com, ~3.2K followers), YouTube (@Tocktify, ~450 subs), tocktify.com, WhatsApp

---

## 1. The honest starting point

Tocktify is a $39/month B2B SaaS product where every seller needs their own
business license and Hormuud merchant account, onboarding is currently
concierge (done by hand, twice ever), and true onboarding capacity is unknown.

That means **the bottleneck is not attention — it is onboarding throughput.**
TikTok boosts at ~$9 per 10–20K views can buy almost unlimited Somali
attention. What cannot be bought is the team's ability to walk hundreds of
sellers through licensing + merchant setup in 7 weeks.

A goal of 10,000 *paying* sellers by Aug 1 would be ~$390K/month of new
revenue from a standing start with manual onboarding. No company has ever done
that. Chasing it would burn the budget on vanity reach and produce churn,
refunds, and a damaged brand in a small, tight-knit market where reputation
is everything.

## 2. The reframed goal: "10,000 in the funnel"

Keep the number. Change what it counts.

> **By August 1: 10,000 people inside the Tocktify funnel** —
> followers + WhatsApp community members + waitlist signups —
> **feeding a Founding 100 cohort of paying sellers.**

| Metric (cumulative, by Aug 1) | Target | Stretch |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok + YouTube followers/subs | 7,000 | 12,000 |
| Waitlist signups (name + WhatsApp + what they sell) | 2,500 | 5,000 |
| WhatsApp community members | 800 | 1,500 |
| Qualified applications (want to pay, ready for KYC) | 300 | 600 |
| **Onboarded paying sellers ("Founding 100")** | **100** | **150** |
| Sellers with ≥1 completed sale | 30 | 60 |
| New MRR | ~$3,900 | ~$6,000 |

Why this is the right target:
- 100 paying, *activated* sellers = 100 success stories = the fuel for the
  real scale phase (Aug–Dec), exactly how Selar grew in Nigeria — creator
  success stories and word-of-mouth, not ad spend.
- A 2,500–5,000 person waitlist means demand is **banked, not wasted**. Every
  person you can't onboard in July is pipeline for August.
- It is achievable with 3 people and $500. The original goal was not, and a
  team that misses by 99% loses morale; a team that hits 10,000-in-funnel and
  100 paying sellers has a fundable, scalable story.

## 3. Positioning

**One-liner (Somali-first market):**
> *"Dukaankaaga online-ka ah — lacagta ku qaado EVC Plus, eDahab iyo Wallet. Tocktify."*
> ("Your online store — get paid with EVC Plus, eDahab and Wallet.")

**The enemy is the DM.** Thousands of Somalis already sell on TikTok,
Instagram and WhatsApp — taking orders in DMs, chasing payment screenshots,
losing track of orders. Tocktify's message is not "start a business"
(hard, slow) — it is **"you already have a business; stop running it in
your DMs"** (instant recognition). This is the #1 audience: existing social
sellers, in Somalia and the diaspora.

**Turn the licensing bottleneck into the offer.** Don't hide the friction —
productize it:

> **The Founding 100 Program**
> We take you from "I sell in my DMs" to a licensed online business:
> we walk you through the business license, set up your Hormuud merchant
> account, build your store with you, and connect EVC Plus/eDahab payouts.
> Founding price locked for life + 2 free months on annual.
> **Only 100 places. Apply now.**

The scarcity is *real* (it's your actual capacity), which makes it honest
marketing and creates urgency. "Done-with-you licensing" converts the
platform's biggest weakness into its most defensible moat — Shopify will
never get a seller a Hormuud merchant account.

**Diaspora wedge — "Buy from Minneapolis, deliver in Mogadishu."**
The diaspora plays three roles: (a) sellers selling into Somalia, (b)
**buyers** — order a Banana Bully cake for your mother in Mogadishu, pay by
card/wallet from abroad, and (c) referrers — "your aunt's tailoring shop
should be on Tocktify." Role (b) is the killer content angle: every
diaspora-buys-for-family video markets the buyer side AND proves the seller
side works. Target geos for boosts: Minneapolis/St. Paul, London, Toronto,
Nairobi (Eastleigh), Stockholm, Columbus OH.

## 4. The funnel

```
TOP        TikTok daily (Somali, EN subs) + boosted winners
           YouTube documentary parts 2–4 + tutorials + Shorts
                          │
CAPTURE    Link-in-bio → tocktify.com/waitlist
           (name, WhatsApp number, "what do you sell?", city)
           Lead magnet: free Somali guide
           "Sida Loo Bilaabo Ganacsi Online ah" (PDF + 5 video lessons)
                          │
MIDDLE     WhatsApp community + broadcast list (the CRM)
           Weekly "Suuq Live" show (TikTok Live + YouTube)
           Tutorial drip, Ahmed/Sahel behind-the-scenes
                          │
BOTTOM     Founding 100 application → onboarding call →
           done-with-you licensing + merchant setup → paying seller
                          │
LOOP       Every onboarded seller = story content + referral
           (1 free month per paying referral, both sides)
```

**WhatsApp is the CRM.** No paid tools. WhatsApp Business app (free):
labels for funnel stage (Lead → Applied → KYC → Live → Selling), catalog
feature for the lead magnet, broadcast lists for the drip. Every waitlist
signup gets a personal WhatsApp message within 24h — at this scale, founders
doing manual outreach IS the growth engine (it's how every B2B SaaS starts).

## 5. Product changes that are marketing (you own the code)

Priority-ordered; items 1–3 should ship in week 1–2:

1. **Public waitlist page with viral referral** — after signing up you get a
   share link; 3 referrals = jump the queue / first month 50% off. Viral
   waitlists routinely produce 1.5–3 signups per organic signup.
2. **Demo store mode (sandbox)** — let anyone build a store and share it
   *before* KYC/license, with payments disabled ("Activate payments" =
   Founding 100 application). This decouples the viral loop from the
   licensing bottleneck — thousands can experience the product while only
   100 go through full onboarding. **This is the single highest-leverage
   change.** It also makes the debated $2 trial work: charge the $2 as a
   "setup deposit" credited to the first month, but don't gate the demo
   store on KYC.
3. **Fix tocktify.com's web presence** — the site currently returns 403 to
   crawlers (check Vercel/WAF bot rules): that blocks Google indexing and
   link previews on WhatsApp/TikTok. Add OpenGraph tags, Somali+English meta
   descriptions, and links to TikTok/YouTube. When someone hears "Tocktify"
   in a video and Googles it, they must find you.
4. **Public storefronts as living demos** — Ahmed's and Sahel's stores
   linked from every video description ("dukaankan wuxuu ku shaqeeyaa
   Tocktify" / "this store runs on Tocktify" badge with referral link).
5. **Referral program in-product** — 1 free month per paying referral, both
   sides. In a community this tight, referral will outperform every ad.

Kill anything above $0.50/signup; reallocate to anything below $0.20.

## 7. Podcast? Verdict: not yet — do a weekly live show instead

A produced podcast is the wrong tool right now: high effort, slow audience
build, no native distribution. Instead run **"Suuq Live"** — a weekly 45–60
min TikTok Live (simulcast/re-upload to YouTube), Somali-language, every
Thursday 8:30pm EAT (catches Somalia evening + diaspora lunchtime US):

- Format: 10 min platform news/demo → 20 min guest seller (start with Ahmed,
  Sahel, then each new Founding 100 seller) → 20 min open Q&A → CTA.
- Each live yields **3–5 clipped shorts** for the daily content quota.
- TikTok Live also drives follows aggressively via the For You feed.
- Revisit a real podcast after August, when you have 20+ seller stories;
  the live show's recordings become its back-catalog.

## 8. Team allocation (3 people + CEO)

| Role | Owner | Load |
|---|---|---|
| Content (shoot/edit 2 posts/day, clips, doc series) | Employee A | full-time |
| Community + funnel (WhatsApp, waitlist follow-up, comments — reply to EVERY comment, many as video replies) | Employee B | full-time |
| Onboarding + seller success (Founding 100 pipeline, licensing SOP, activation to first sale) | Employee C | full-time |
| Suuq Live host, partnerships, boost decisions, weekly metrics | CEO | recurring |

**Week 1 critical task for Employee C:** onboard 3 sellers end-to-end and
*time every step*. You cannot plan capacity you haven't measured. Write the
SOP (checklist: license → Hormuud merchant → SMS gateway → store → test
payment) as you go; the SOP becomes both your operations manual and a
tutorial content series.

## 9. KPIs — review every Monday

Weekly scorecard (one row per week): TikTok views / new followers / YouTube
subs / waitlist adds / WhatsApp members / applications / sellers onboarded /
sellers with a sale / MRR / $ spent / cost per waitlist signup.

Leading indicator that matters most: **waitlist adds per week**. If that's
on pace (≈350/wk average), the Aug 1 goal lands.

## 10. Risks

- **Onboarding slower than hoped** → the waitlist banks the demand; shrink
  "Founding 100" to "Founding 50" publicly only if week-3 data forces it
  (under-promise beats missing).
- **Licensing/Hormuud delays outside your control** → demo store mode keeps
  momentum; sellers wait *inside* the product, not outside it.
- **Boost fatigue / falling CPMs efficiency** → the decision rule in §6;
  organic + referral should overtake paid by week 4.
- **Two-seller proof is thin** → that's *why* the Founding 100 are
  onboarded concierge-style: each one must succeed and become content.
